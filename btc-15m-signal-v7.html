V7 improvements
• BRTI calibration anchor means you no longer need to retype BRTI every few seconds.
• The Coinbase→BRTI offset is carried forward and gradually loses confidence as it ages.
• The model tightens its required edge as expiration approaches.
• During the final 60 seconds, it tracks a second-by-second estimated settlement average rather than looking only at the last tick.
• No new entry is allowed in the final 30 seconds.
• Quotes and target are saved locally so accidental refreshes do not wipe the contract.
