What gets logged?
At most once every 10 seconds: contract, time remaining, target source, proxy price, adjusted proxy, raw/calibrated probability, market odds, BRTI basis state, edges, signal, data quality, volatility and dispersion. Manual Robinhood snapshots record BRTI and normalized UP odds beside the model. Resolutions can be imported from Kalshi when available or marked manually.
Model discipline
V9.7 never converts Robinhood or Kalshi market odds into the model probability used to claim edge. Spot/settlement math produces the probability; Kalshi/Polymarket are cross-checks and execution filters. Missing feeds increase uncertainty or force PASS. When Kalshi is unavailable, the settlement model still runs from the Robinhood target and robust cross-venue spot proxy, but execution remains PASS because no live Kalshi quote exists. Resilience penalties shrink model confidence toward 50% when venue count, freshness, dispersion, volatility regime, prediction-market availability, history depth, or mean-reversion risk deteriorate. The direct CF Benchmarks BRTI feed is not embedded because it requires authenticated/licensed access.
