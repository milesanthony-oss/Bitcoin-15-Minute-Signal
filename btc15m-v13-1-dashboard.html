Calibration buckets: —
V10.7 uses settlement-progress calibration and learns additional logit scaling only after at least five resolved contracts exist in a time bucket. Official outcomes are imported automatically when available. Final-minute probability now uses locked one-second settlement observations and forecasts only the unknown remainder.
What gets logged?
At most once every 10 seconds: contract, time remaining, target source, proxy price, adjusted proxy, raw/calibrated probability, market odds, BRTI basis state, edges, signal, data quality, volatility and dispersion. Manual Robinhood snapshots record BRTI and normalized UP odds beside the model. Resolutions can be imported from Kalshi when available or marked manually.
Model discipline
V12.3 keeps the settlement model independent, preserves the execution-side lock, and automatically captures qualifying BET flashes once the Robinhood target/window is entered and verified. Model direction can change freely, but a Robinhood quote is permanently associated with the locked UP or DOWN side. Opposite-side movement cancels the entry workflow, clears the quote, and requires a fresh stability period before a new side can be priced. The user enters Robinhood's exact target and verifies the 15-minute window; the model uses that target. DepthFeed/Kalshi/Polymarket remain external confirmation and are automatically excluded from decision support when the external target differs materially. Robinhood execution price remains manual-only. External quotes are never relabeled as Robinhood executable prices. Missing or stale external feeds, an unverified Robinhood contract, or a missing/stale manual Robinhood price forces WATCH/PASS. The direct CF Benchmarks BRTI feed is not embedded because it requires authenticated/licensed access.
