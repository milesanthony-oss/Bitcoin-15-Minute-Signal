What gets logged?
At most once every 10 seconds: contract, time remaining, target source, proxy price, raw/calibrated probability, market odds, edges, signal, data-quality score, volatility and dispersion. After a Kalshi contract reports a result, matching snapshots are automatically labeled for Brier score and hit-rate analysis.
Model discipline
V9 never converts an external market quote into the model probability used to claim edge. Spot/settlement math produces the probability; Kalshi/Polymarket are cross-checks and execution filters. Missing feeds increase uncertainty or force PASS. When Kalshi is unavailable, the settlement model still runs from the Robinhood target and robust cross-venue spot proxy, but execution remains PASS because no live Kalshi quote exists. The direct CF Benchmarks BRTI feed is not embedded because it requires authenticated/licensed access.
