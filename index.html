Automatic protections
• Coinbase prices are resampled to one observation per second before momentum/volatility calculations.
• No minimum win-probability gate: a 59% outcome at 40¢ can qualify if its EV clears the required edge.
• Probability is automatically shrunk toward 50% as the BRTI anchor ages.
• Quote freshness increases the required edge and eventually blocks entries.
• The contract automatically advances after expiry and clears stale target/quotes.
• Final 60 seconds use the developing settlement average; final 30 seconds remain entry-locked.
• Wide or inconsistent UP/DOWN pricing blocks marginal trades.
